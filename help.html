The goal: spell F‑L‑I‑P

Each round is a race to get rid of all your cards. The first player out takes 2 letters, the second takes 1, and that ends the round. Whoever spells the whole word first wins.

FLIP

Your turn

If your set from last turn is still on the table, it's cleared away first (more on that below). Then you do one of these four things.

1Play a set

One or more cards of the same number. If someone already has a set the same size out, yours must be higher, and theirs goes back to their hand, turned over.

2Add a card to someone's set

It must be the same number. The set gets one bigger, so it may now clash with another set of that size: it has to beat it, and the beaten set goes back.

3Take someone's set

The whole set comes into your hand, and every card turns over on the way. Handy when the other side makes a great new group.

4Flip your hand

Every card in your hand turns over at once, and it uses up your turn. In the game, the first tap on Flip hand is a free preview: your hand turns over just on your phone, with a summary of what you'd hold. Then choose Flip for real or Cancel. You can preview on anyone's turn to plan ahead.

Which sets clash?

Sets only fight sets of the same size. A single only cares about singles, a pair only about pairs, and so on. There's never more than one set of each size on the table. Pick a set to play against this table:

Your set is cleared at the start of your next turn

If nobody beats or takes your set while it goes round the table, it's discarded as your next turn begins. Those cards are gone for good, which is how you shed cards.

Going out

Play or add your last card and you're out. The first player out takes 2 letters; their last set stays on the table until their turn would come round, and if someone beats it in the meantime it's simply discarded. The second player out takes 1 letter and the round ends. Everyone's cards are gathered, shuffled and dealt again.

The deck

There are 40 cards. Fewer players use fewer cards, so everyone always gets the same size hand. The game sorts this out for you; the pips on the edge of a card show when it joins in.

Tips for the phone

  • Slide along your hand to magnify cards. Lift your finger to pick the card under it; lift on more cards of the same number to build a set.
  • Tap the bar under a group ("all 3") to pick all your matching cards at once. Matching numbers always sit together.
  • Read the line above your hand: green means your set goes down, red tells you what it needs.
  • Tap the table to hurry the animations along.
  • Running low on good numbers? Tap Flip hand for a free preview of your hand turned over. The line above your hand sums up what you'd hold and whether your best set would beat what's on the table. Nobody else sees it, and you can do it while others are playing.
  • Computer players can fill empty seats: Relaxed ones play loosely, Sharp ones play to win and will happily take your sets.
